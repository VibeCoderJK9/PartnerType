Lead stages
Intake stages (fixed)
Before a lead enters your funnel you decide whether to accept it. These four stages cannot be changed — each one fires a notification and a log entry.
Your sales funnel
You define these stages yourself — a partner moves their lead through them on the board. Commission is shown at every stage, but only becomes payable after a win. Changes are saved automatically when you leave a field, confirmed by a “Saved” marker.
    Final stages (fixed)
    Every sale ends at either Won or Lost. Won sends the commission for settlement, while Lost closes the lead without commission. The names, colours and roles of these two stages are fixed.
