# Code of conduct

We want PartnerType to be a friendly place to contribute, whatever your experience, background or
language.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and help newcomers.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or personal attacks.
- No publishing of other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **kontakt@partnertype.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor.

This code is based on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
